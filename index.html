The vision

A village you can actually move to.

We are building a network of families who choose to relocate and settle near or within free cities in Europe, while remaining connected through shared values, mutual support, and group adventures further afield.

Our focus is simple: raise children with freedom, grow real food, create resilient neighbourhoods, and live among people who share the same desire for independence and community. Not a forum. Not a someday-plan. Families with boxes packed and hands in the soil.

What we stand for

What we stand for

Four roots we grow from.

Homeschooling & unschooling

Children learn through life, curiosity, and real responsibility rather than institutional systems.

Gardening & food production

Greens, vegetables and fruit grown by hand — soil health and greater self-reliance at the household and village level.

Community living

A true neighbourhood of families who know and support one another, not isolated households.

Free-city alignment

Settling within or close to places that prioritise liberty, low bureaucracy, and voluntary cooperation.

European anchors

Three places we are oriented toward.

Different climates, legal environments, and practical opportunities — each reachable for families seeking a European base.

Danube · Serbia & Croatia

Liberland

A river frontier on the Danube — young, pioneering, and building from first principles with people who chose to be there.

Southern Norway

Liberstad

Forest, land and long northern summers — a private city project in Norway with room to build, plant and raise children outdoors.

Montenegro

Montelibero

Mediterranean climate, mountains and sea — a growing free-market community where a family can garden nearly year-round.

Beyond daily life

We travel together, too.

Group holidays and longer stays in other free-city projects — such as Prospera and Morazán in Honduras — are part of the vision, so the community stays connected even when exploring further afield.

  • Prospera, Roatán
  • Morazán, Honduras
  • Shared winter stays
  • Family-scale trips

Children who grow up crossing borders with the same friends learn something no classroom hands out: the world is open, and it belongs to those who go and see it.

Who we are

Started by a family, for families.

Librevore is started by Tania and Sondre, parents of two young children, ages 1 and 6. We are building this because we want a better environment for our family — and we know we are not the only ones.

We are actively looking for more families who share the same direction: people ready to move, build, grow food, educate their children differently, and create a real village culture rather than just talk about it.
